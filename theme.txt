CRITICAL BUSINESS MODULE ADDENDUM
Elite Washwo — Surf Complete Stock, Return, Expense & Salesman Ledger
The system must NOT be considered complete unless the following modules and business logic are implemented correctly.
The main product is:
Surf
The business works through Salesmen/Bookers who receive stock from Elite Washwo, sell it to customers, collect money, and may return unsold/damaged stock at the end of the day.
The system must maintain a complete and accurate financial + stock ledger for every salesman.
